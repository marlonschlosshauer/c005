# Cadence

The language used for course purchases and refunds.

## Language

**Course purchase**:
A purchaser's payment for one or more seats on a particular course. Separate purchases of the same course have independent refund eligibility.

**Purchaser**:
The person who paid for a course purchase, who alone may request a return of its payment and receive the returned money. The purchaser may be different from the person using a seat.

**Seat**:
An entitlement for one person to access a course, bought as part of a course purchase. Only a successfully completed refund payment removes that seat's access, even when only part of its payment is returned.

**Refund**:
A return of all or part of one seat's fixed share of the original course purchase payment, including discounts, with the amount determined by a reviewer and capped at that share; shares are equal apart from consistently allocated currency rounding. A refund request asks for that return and does not mean the payment has already been returned.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancel my purchase

**Refund request**:
A purchaser's submission asking for all or part of the payment for exactly one purchased seat to be returned, subject to eligibility barriers at submission rather than at decision. A pending or approved request blocks another request for that seat, including after partial approval or while payment is pending; a new submission after rejection is a separate request.

**Refund decision**:
A refund reviewer's final approval or rejection of a refund request, identifying the reviewer and the approved amount where applicable; approval does not mean the payment has been returned. The purchaser can see the decision, approved amount, and any optional explanation.

**Refund reviewer**:
An admin authorized to approve or reject refund requests.
