# Cadence

The language used for course purchases and refunds.

## Language

**Course purchase**:
A purchaser's payment for one or more seats on a particular course. Separate purchases of the same course have independent refund eligibility.

**Purchaser**:
The person who paid for a course purchase, who alone may request a return of its payment and receive the returned money. The purchaser may be different from the person using a seat.

**Seat**:
An entitlement for one person to access a course, bought as part of a course purchase. A refunded seat no longer grants access to the course, even when only part of its payment is returned.

**Refund**:
A return of all or part of the original payment for one purchased seat, with the amount determined by a reviewer. A refund request asks for that return and does not mean the payment has already been returned.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancel my purchase

**Refund request**:
A purchaser's submission asking for all or part of the payment for exactly one purchased seat to be returned, which may be approved or rejected. A new submission after rejection is a separate request.

**Refund decision**:
An admin's final approval or rejection of a refund request, identifying the admin and the approved amount where applicable. An explanation may accompany the decision but is optional.
